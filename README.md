# MCF-Launchpad-Project
A project created with my team at the Mark Cuban AI Bootcamp.

## Project Overview
This project, completed as part of the Launchpad course, analyzes and reduces selection bias in a public census dataset from 1994. 
Although this was a team project, I was the lead contributor and completed the majority of the coding, resampling, and presentation, ensuring the reproducibility and clarity of the results. 

## Goal
-Identify and reduce biases in the UCI Adult Income Dataset.
-Apply resampling techniques to reduce gender and racial bias.
-Demonstrate the effects of these corrections through analysis and visualizations.
-Prevent future AI models from learning from biased data. 

## My Role
-Lead contributor: handled data cleaning, resampling, and analysis.
-Developed the Colab notebook for reproducibility. 
-Generated visualizations and graphs to demonstrate bias reduction. 
-Created most of the presentation shared on demo day.

## Dataset
UCI Adult Income Dataset
The dataset includes demographic attributes such as age, sex, race, education, and income.
Certain demographic groups, such as sex and race, were underrepresented or overrepresented, leading to biased distributions. 

This dataset is included in this repository to ensure reproducibility.
